## Idea Title
IMU selection: TDK ICM-45686 replaces ICM-42688-P — the 4,000 °/s range match

## Summary (2-3 lines)
The sim's `GYRO_SAT_RPS = 4,000 °/s` design constant has **no part on the ICM-42688-P**:
its gyro FSR tops at **±2000 dps** (datasheet Table 1). The ICM-45686 (DS-000577, 2024)
offers **±4000 dps**, ±0.3 °/s initial ZRO (vs ±0.5), 0.0038 °/s/√Hz RND, ±32 g accel
(vs ±16 g), 70–110 µg/√Hz accel RND, and the same **20,000 g unpowered shock
(0.2 ms)** that covers the 15,000 g setback. Same 2.5×3 mm LGA-14, 24 MHz SPI,
−40…+85 °C.

## Evidence
- ICM-42688-P gyro FSR list: ±15.625…±2000 dps only (Table 1, §4.6) — the config.py
  comment "ICM-42688 saturation" at 4000 °/s does not match that part; it matches the
  ICM-45686. IIM-42352 is a 3-axis **accelerometer** (no gyro — not an IMU option).
  BMI088 gyro tops at ±2000 dps — fails the same gate (and its accel is ±24 g class).
- Shock: **both** 42688-P and 45686 are rated 20,000 g for 0.2 ms unpowered → setback
  (15,000 g design peak) is survivable powered-off with 1.33× margin; potting QA
  (BOM A10 pot) retained as assembly insurance. 22× gyro saturation pre-despin remains
  accepted by design (report §4.5).

## Pros / Cons
- Pros: exact 4,000 °/s FS match to the GNC constant; lower bias spec than baseline;
  ±32 g accel covers servo-slew transients with margin; 20,000 g shock; 2024 part
  (long availability); same package/pinout class → drop-in board change.
- Cons: RND 0.0038 vs 0.0028 °/s/√Hz (36% worse noise — irrelevant: sim assumes 0.2 °/s
  per-sample incl. vibration, ~50× the sensor-only figure); newer part = higher price
  (≈US$4–5 vs ≈US$3–4 @1k).

## Cost Estimate (lakhs)
≈₹450 @1k (≈US$4.5) — D3 board BOM impact negligible (±₹150 vs baseline chip).

## Recommendation
**Select ICM-45686.** Runner-up ICM-42688-P (cheaper, better RND, fails 4,000 °/s gate).
